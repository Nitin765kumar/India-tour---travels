Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

At IndiaTours, we prioritize the privacy of our travelers. This policy outlines how we collect and protect your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and travel preferences when you book a trip or subscribe to our newsletter.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to:

  • Process and confirm your travel bookings.
  • Provide 24/7 technical and travel support.
  • Send updates regarding your itinerary or heritage stories.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures (SSL encryption) to ensure your personal data is protected from unauthorized access or disclosure.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share necessary details with verified travel partners (hotels, local guides, transport) strictly to fulfill your booking requirements.
